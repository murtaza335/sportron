Why Sportron?

Sportron was built for the community that loves to play — students, teachers, and players who want a reliable way to find partners and keep score.

🏸 Play more

Find open pickup sessions near you and jump in.

📊 Track every match

Record scores, get results approved by your opponent, and watch your record build.

🎯 Stay in your lane

Join communities by sport, from table tennis to football.

🔔 Never miss a game

Get pinged when there's live action in your community, and get notified when plans change.

Features

Communities

  • Start or join communities organized around a sport
  • Public (join anytime) or restricted (admin approves)
  • Discover by sport or search by name
  • Cover photos, member lists, and admin tools

Match Tracking

  • Record matches inside a community — singles or doubles
  • Team sports: add team names and opponent captain
  • Score with +/- stepper and direct entry
  • Opponent approval: confirm or challenge results
  • Likes, comments, win/loss records

Find a Match

  • Pickup sessions with sport, location, time, and capacity
  • Sessions expire automatically if no one joins
  • Apply, accept, reject, or leave

Pings

  • Ping your whole community
  • Members reply Coming or Busy, live

Notifications

  • Real-time alerts for applications, approvals, pings, invites
  • Push notifications; each type customizable

Privacy First

We take your data seriously.

  • No GPS tracking — the app uses text locations you type yourself
  • Google sign-in only — we never store your password
  • No ads, no selling data
  • In-app Delete Account — erase your data anytime

How to Use

  1. Sign in with your Google account — no separate sign-up needed.
  2. Complete your profile — username, bio, role, photo.
  3. Explore communities via the Communities tab or Discover.
  4. Join or create a community — membership is required to record matches.
  5. Record a match from within a community so your opponent can approve it.
  6. Find a Match tab — create or apply to a pickup session.
  7. Manage notifications in Settings for what you want to hear.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sportron free?

Yes — Sportron is free to download and use.

Do I need an account?

You sign in with Google. A profile is created the first time you sign in.

What sports are supported?

Table tennis, badminton, tennis, squash, pickleball, football, cricket, volleyball, handball, and basketball — plus a generic "Other".

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account to permanently remove your data.
