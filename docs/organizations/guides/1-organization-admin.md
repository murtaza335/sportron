# Sportron at NUST: organization admin (DD Sports)

You decide who can organize, you look after the venue screens, and you can manage every NUST tournament.

## Where to start
Open Sportron (app or web), sign in, tap the **Tournaments** tab, then **Organize · NUST**.

## Add or remove organizers
1. Tap **Organizers**, then **Add organizer**.
2. Search their Sportron username and tap it. They get a notification.
3. To remove someone, tap **Remove**. Their access ends at once. Their tournaments stay with NUST.

Other organization admins are added or removed by the Sportron team.

## Set up a venue screen (once per TV)
1. Tap **Screens**, then **Add screen**. Name it, for example *Old Gym TV*.
2. Open the screen's link on the computer connected to the TV and press **F11**.
3. Leave it running. You never need to touch the TV again.

## Choose what a screen shows
Open the screen, tap **Choose a tournament** (or **Change**) and pick one. If another tournament is already showing, you are asked **Replace?** and told who put it there. **Courts on this screen** limits a TV to some courts. When nothing is running the TV shows a NUST waiting page.

## Tournaments
- **All tournaments** lists every NUST tournament. You can edit any of them.
- **Hide tournament** (on its manage page) removes it from players and screens without deleting it.
- Published tournaments can be cancelled, not deleted. Drafts can be deleted. To delete a published one for good, contact the Sportron team.

## Activity
**Activity** shows what changed and who did it: tournaments created, published or cancelled, people added or removed, screens changed.

## Players' contact details
You and the organizers of a tournament can see players' phone numbers, emails and CMS IDs. Use them only to run the event. Scorers and the public screens never show them.
