# Sportron at NUST: scorer

You score matches. Two taps get you to a match.

## Before you start
Charge your phone. Open Sportron and sign in. Stay signed in.

## Score a match
1. **Tournaments** tab → **To score**.
2. Tap your match.
3. Check the **court**, choose **who serves first**, tap **Start**.
4. **Tap a side** for each point. Made a mistake? **Undo** (bottom left).
5. When the match is over tap **Finish**, check the result and tap **Save result**.

The result counts at once. After saving you cannot change it: tell the sport manager.

## Helpful
- **Lock screen** (menu) stops accidental taps. Hold for one second to unlock.
- Doubles: *player 1* of each pair is the one who serves or receives first.
- Basketball: the clock stops itself at 0:00. Hold a counter to take one away.

## No internet?
Keep scoring. Everything is saved on your phone and sent when you are back online. Keep the app open until the top of the screen shows it is synced.

## Someone else is on your match
The app tells you who. Tap **Take over scoring** only if they have stopped (dead phone, left the court). Their phone then stops scoring.

## "You can no longer score this match"
You were removed or the organization is paused. Your points are still on your phone; ask the sport manager.
