# Privacy policy — draft addition for tournaments (for owner approval)

> Drafted by the implementer. NOT applied to `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` — review, edit, then paste in.

## Tournament participant information

When you take part in a tournament organised through Sportron, a tournament organiser (a tournament
or module administrator) may enter details about you so that you can be registered and contacted.
Depending on the form the organiser configures, this can include your **name, CMS/student ID,
school or department, phone number, email address** and other answers the organiser asks for
(for example department or T-shirt size).

- **Who can see it.** Your name and school, and any field the organiser marks public, are visible to
  other signed-in users on the tournament page. Your CMS ID, phone number, email and any private
  fields are visible only to the tournament's administrators and, if the organiser linked your
  Sportron account to the entry, to you.
- **Why we process it.** To run the event: building brackets and standings, scheduling matches,
  recording results and contacting participants.
- **Notifications.** If an organiser links your account to an entry, you may receive notifications
  about your matches, schedule and results. You can turn push notifications off in Settings.
- **Retention and deletion.** Tournament records (including results and the participant details above)
  are kept by the organiser for as long as the tournament exists. To have your details corrected or
  removed from a tournament, contact the tournament organiser or Sportron support at the contact
  address in this policy.
- **Third parties.** Organisers are responsible for having a lawful basis to enter other people's
  details. We do not sell this information.

## Addition for v2: team rosters

For team and doubles events, the organiser enters the details above **for every player on a team**
(not only one contact person), plus an optional team captain and squad number. The same visibility
rules apply per player: a player's name and school are visible to other signed-in users; their CMS ID,
phone number and email are visible only to the tournament's administrators and to that player (if the
organiser linked their Sportron account). Teammates do **not** see each other's private details.
Linked players receive the notifications their team receives (schedule, results, completion).
