## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the App (a multi-sport social networking application for communities, sessions, and match tracking) and to provide you with the features you choose to use.

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Google account profile:** When you sign in using Google, we obtain your Google account name and email address and use it to create your account. We do not access your Google password.
- **Profile information:** Your chosen username, bio, and optional photo, which you provide and manage yourself.
- **Profile photo / community cover photos:** Photos you upload. These are stored and served by our image-processing provider (Cloudinary) and displayed to other users where relevant.
- **Community content:** Community names, descriptions, and the sport each community is linked to.
- **User-generated content:** Details you post and share — including recording a match, creating a session, posting a ping, writing comments, and adding likes. Depending on the feature, this content may be visible to other members of the relevant community or to the public.
- **Match and session content:** Details you enter when recording a match or creating a session, including sport(s) played, scores, team, versus/opponent names, and participating members.
- **Messages and pings:** Text you send when pinging a community and your "Coming"/"Busy" responses.
- **Venue/location text:** When you create a session, you may type the name or location of a place. **We do not access your device's GPS or location services, and we do not track your physical location.**

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Registration tokens (device token):** To deliver push notifications, we receive a unique token assigned to your device by the notification service (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and store it under our profile record.
- **Transactional metadata:** System-generated timestamps (e.g., when you joined a community, recorded a match, or performed an action), which are stored in our database.
- **Usage logs:** We do not deploy third-party advertising or, at this time, dedicated analytics SDKs. Our cloud infrastructure providers may maintain standard operational logs for security and availability with their services.

### 2.3 Information We Do Not Collect
- We do **not** collect your device's real-time location or GPS coordinates.
- We do **not** request access to your contacts, SMS, microphone, camera roll (files are uploaded only when you explicitly select a photo), or files.
- We do **not** sell or rent your personal data.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only for the following purposes:

1. **Provide the service:** Create and manage your account, authenticate you via Google.
2. **Communities & sessions:** Administer communities, membership, join requests, invites, sessions, and match records you participate in.
3. **Matches & social features:** Record matches, display scores and standings, process likes and comments, and generate per-community statistics that you ask for.
4. **Notifications:** Send you in-app and push notifications for actions (e.g., session requests, join requests, match approvals, pings, invites). You can disable them at any time in the Settings.
5. **Security & integrity:** Detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, security incidents, and prohibited content—consistent with our Terms of Service.
6. **Legal compliance:** Respond to lawful requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

### Lawful bases
Where required, we rely on the following bases under applicable law:
- **Consent**, where you have explicitly agreed (e.g., push notifications and contributing content).
- **Performance of a contract/service**, to operate the features you have signed up for.
- **Legitimate interests**, such as service integrity and security, that do not override your rights.

## 4. Sharing of Your Information

We do **not** sell your information. We share it only with the categories of recipients outlined below, and only as necessary to operate the features you use.

1. **Service providers (data processors):**
   - **Supabase** — cloud database and authentication provider that stores the App's records and hosts its data.
   - **Google / Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)** – delivering push notifications.
   - **Cloudinary** – storage, optimisation, and delivery of the photos you upload (profile pictures and community covers).
   These providers process data only in accordance with our instructions and are bound by appropriate confidentiality and processing terms.

2. **Authorities and regulators:** Where we are legally required to disclose information (e.g., in response to a court order, law enforcement request, or in compliance with the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016), we will disclose only the minimum necessary and solely for the stated purpose.

3. **Other users:** By the nature of the App, certain content you choose to share is visible to other users (e.g., your username, community membership, community match feed, pings, and comments).
   - In **public communities**, your community profile and public content may be visible to anyone who joins or browses.
   - In **Restricted (invite/approval-only) communities**, interactions are limited to other approved members only. Members must be invited or approved by an administrator, and you can limit your community accordingly.
   - We do **not** make user-generated content available for public viewing on any website or third-party platform outside the App.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for advertising, marketing, or any other commercial purpose.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to:

## 7. Your Rights

You have the following rights, subject to the legal grounds:

- **Access and portability:** Receive a copy of the information we hold about you.
- **Correction:** Update or correct inaccurate personal information (you can edit your username, bio, and photo in Profile).
- **Deletion:** Request that we delete your account and associated records (see Section 6).
- **Restriction / objection:** Request that we limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.
- **Withdrawal of consent:** Withdraw consent provided for specific processing (e.g., push notifications) at any time in Settings; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.
- **Erasure:** Where processing is based solely on your consent, request that we erase your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the contact details in Section 1. We will respond without reasonable delay, and in any event within the timelines applicable under Pakistani law. We may request you to verify your identity before undertaking any request.

**Residents of Pakistan:** You may also lodge a complaint with the competent authorities (including the Federal Investigation Agency's Cybercrime Wing / relevant data-protection body) if you believe your rights have been violated.

## 9. Security

We take commercially reasonable steps to protect your information:

- All sign-in flows use **Google OAuth** (we never receive or store your Google password).
- Data is transmitted in **encrypted form (TLS/HTTPS)** between your device and our servers.
- Access control policies (including role-based permissions) are applied to our database to limit what each authenticated user can view or modify.
- We keep our infrastructure up to date and review access to production.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% safe. While we strive to use reasonable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security in connection with any breach or abuse.
